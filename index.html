Textile Optics & Armocromia Science

How Fabric Reflectance Alters Color Perception

1. Specular vs. Diffuse Scattering

Smooth filament silks and satins produce sharp specular highlights, intensifying chromatic contrast and reflecting light back onto the jawline.

2. Surface Micro-Shadows

Linen slubs, tweed bouclé, and brushed cashmere create micro-shadows that diffuse glare, forgiving low-contrast and muted skin tones.

3. Temperature & Fiber Undertone

Unbleached natural linen and camel wool carry organic golden warmth, whereas mercerized cotton and synthetic filament silks lean neutral-to-cool.
